About Alumni Connect

Bridging the gap between graduates and their alma mater.

Our Mission

Alumni Connect was built to help every graduate of our engineering college stay in touch with their peers, mentors and juniors long after they've left campus.

From reconnecting with old classmates to discovering job referrals and attending reunions, this platform is the digital home for our alumni community.

Whether you graduated last year or two decades ago, there's always a reason to come back and give back.
